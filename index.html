Meet Your Doctor

Dr. Mohamed El Zayat

Obstetrics & Gynaecology · Internal Medicine

Dr. El Zayat founded Mama Salone Hospital with one belief: that every family in Sierra Leone deserves healthcare that is skilled, honest and kind. He is known for taking the time to explain, to reassure, and to treat each patient as a neighbour rather than a number.

“I want every mother to feel safe, every child to feel brave, and every family to leave our hospital knowing exactly what happens next.”
