Our Departments

Everything explained, clearly

Here is exactly what each department offers, so you know where to go and what to expect.

Obstetrics & Gynaecology

Women's health is at the heart of Mama Salone Hospital. Whether you are expecting your first baby, managing a gynaecological concern, or simply coming in for a routine check, you will be cared for by an experienced team who understand both the medicine and the emotions involved.

What's included

  • Antenatal (pregnancy) care and routine scans
  • Safe delivery and postnatal follow-up for mother and baby
  • Family planning and contraception counselling
  • Treatment of fibroids, ovarian cysts and infections
  • Fertility guidance and referrals
  • Cervical screening and well-woman check-ups
Book this service

Pediatric Clinic

Children are not small adults — they need gentle hands, patient explanations and a doctor who can put them at ease. Our pediatric clinic treats newborns through teenagers, with a focus on prevention as much as cure.

What's included

  • Newborn examinations and growth monitoring
  • Routine immunisations and vaccination schedules
  • Treatment of malaria, typhoid, respiratory infections and diarrhoea
  • Nutritional assessment and feeding advice
  • School-age health checks and developmental guidance
  • Emergency care for children, seven days a week
Book this service

Internal Medicine

Internal medicine is the department that looks at the whole picture. Whether you are managing a long-term condition or trying to understand symptoms that have been worrying you, Dr. El Zayat and the clinical team will investigate thoroughly and explain clearly.

What's included

  • Diagnosis and treatment of malaria, typhoid and other fevers
  • Long-term management of diabetes and hypertension
  • Management of asthma and chronic respiratory conditions
  • Digestive, stomach and liver complaints
  • Routine check-ups and health screening
  • Referrals to specialists when needed
Book this service

Dental Clinic

Dental pain stops everything. Our dental clinic offers calm, sterile care for adults and children — with unhurried explanations and gentle technique for patients who have been nervous about dentists in the past.

What's included

  • Dental check-ups and professional cleaning
  • Fillings for cavities and tooth decay
  • Tooth extractions, including difficult removals
  • Treatment of toothache and gum infections
  • Advice on brushing, diet and children's dental health
  • Referrals for orthodontics and advanced procedures
Book this service

Laboratory

Accurate diagnosis starts with accurate testing. Our on-site laboratory means most results are ready the same day — so treatment can begin immediately instead of after a long wait.

What's included

  • Malaria and typhoid testing
  • Full blood count, blood group and haemoglobin
  • Blood sugar and diabetes monitoring
  • Urine and stool analysis
  • Pregnancy testing and antenatal screening
  • Liver, kidney and infection panels
Book this service

Pharmacy

Having a pharmacy inside the hospital means you never have to leave with a prescription in your hand and no idea where to fill it. Our pharmacists dispense genuine, affordable medicines and take time to explain how to use them correctly.

What's included

  • Full range of prescription medicines
  • Antimalarials, antibiotics and pain relief
  • Medicines for chronic conditions such as diabetes and hypertension
  • Children's medicines in appropriate formulations
  • Clear dosage instructions and counselling
  • Antenatal vitamins and family planning supplies

What To Expect

Frequently asked questions

If your question isn't answered here, please call us — we're happy to help.

Walk-ins are always welcome, and emergencies are seen immediately. However, booking an appointment means shorter waiting times and lets us prepare for your visit — especially for antenatal care, dental work or specialist consultations.

Consultation fees are affordable and clearly displayed at reception. If additional tests, medicines or procedures are needed, you will be told the cost before any treatment begins — no surprises.

Most routine tests — including malaria, blood count and blood sugar — are ready the same day. Some specialised panels may take longer, and you will be told at the time of testing.

Please bring any previous medical records, test results or prescriptions, a list of medicines you are currently taking, and — for antenatal visits — your antenatal card if you have one.

Yes. We are open seven days a week — Saturday 9:00–18:00 and Sunday 10:00–16:00. Emergency support is always available by phone on any of our four lines.

Not sure which department you need?

Call us and we will guide you to the right service — or book a general consultation with Dr. El Zayat, who will assess you and refer you as needed.
